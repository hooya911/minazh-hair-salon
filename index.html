About

Hi, I'm Minazh.

My studio is at home. One chair, a tall lit mirror, and a coffee while I work. It's quiet and private, and I'm not running between three people at once. We start by talking about what you actually want, including the parts that are hard to put into words. Then I take the time it needs.

I care a lot about hair staying healthy. If a colour is going to wreck it, I'll say so, and we'll find another way to get you there.

Bring your kids. Bring your before photos. Bring the screenshot you've had saved for six months. All of it helps.
