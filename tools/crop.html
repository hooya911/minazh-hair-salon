Photo cropper

Crops a photo to the exact shape a slot on the site needs and saves it with the right filename. Everything happens in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Pick the slot

2. Add the photo

Paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac.

You can also drag an image file here, or

Drag the box to move it. Drag the circle to resize.

3. Then

Your browser drops the cropped files in Downloads. When you have the ones you want, upload them to the images folder on GitHub and they'll appear on the site.

  1. Crop as many as you like. Each one saves with the filename the site expects.
  2. Upload them all at once to images/ in the repo.
  3. Photos you skip keep showing the warm colour wash, so the page still looks finished.
